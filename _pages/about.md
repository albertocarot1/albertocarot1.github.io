## Alberto Caroli

I’m Alberto Caroli, a drummer from [town], Italy, with two decades of live and studio work. I play with Balera (doom/sludge), Effigy (grunge/stoner), Sand Monolith (doom) and ZEMIC (indie rock), and take on live and session work for other artists. I’ve opened for Messa, Savana Funk, Elli de Mon and Herba Mate, played the Dino Manuzzi stadium with Rockin’ 1000, and the San Marino Eurovision 2025 selection with Reagents. I record, mix and master in my own studio, so I can hand you drum tracks ready to drop into your session, or a finished mix.
