  <h1>Privacy notice</h1>

  <h2>Who is responsible?</h2>
  <p>The data controller is Alberto Caroli. You can contact me through the <a href="/#contact">contact form</a>.</p>

  <h2>What I collect and why</h2>
  <p>If you use the contact form, I receive the name, email address, optional phone number, project type and message you provide. I use these details to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss or arrange work. Formspree processes and delivers form submissions for me.</p>
  <p>The usual legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract. Alberto Caroli should confirm the correct legal basis before publication. Formspree may also process technical details needed to deliver and protect submissions; its current privacy terms should be checked.</p>
  <p>If you choose to book a call, Calendly receives the name, email and scheduling details you enter on its booking page to arrange the meeting. Calendly is an external service; its own privacy notice applies there.</p>
  <p>If you start a chat through WhatsApp or Telegram, those services receive your account details and messages. Their own privacy terms apply to those chats.</p>
  <p>Pages with embedded YouTube, Spotify or SoundCloud players load content from those providers. They may receive technical information, set cookies or collect usage data when the player loads or is used. This site also loads its fonts from Google Fonts. No analytics service is installed in this site's source; Alberto Caroli should check whether the embeds need consent controls before publication.</p>

  <h2>How long I keep it</h2>
  <p>I keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting work, then delete them unless I need to keep records for legal reasons. Alberto Caroli: set a specific retention period and confirm the retention settings of the providers above.</p>

  <h2>Your choices and rights</h2>
  <p>You can ask me to access, correct or delete your personal information, or raise another privacy request, using the contact form and writing “privacy request” in your message. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Any applicable rights depend on your location and circumstances.</p>

  <h2>Third-party services</h2>
  <p>Formspree handles contact-form submissions. Calendly handles bookings you make through its site. WhatsApp and Telegram handle chats you start with them. YouTube, Spotify and SoundCloud provide embedded media, and Google Fonts provides typefaces. These services may process data under their own privacy terms; Alberto Caroli should verify their current data-processing terms and transfer arrangements before publication.</p>
